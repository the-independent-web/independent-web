# Welcome to The Independent Web

The web is more interesting when everyone has a place of their own.

For a long time, the web was full of personal websites, small
communities, blogs, forums, and strange little projects. You could
find something interesting simply by following links from one place
to another.

The web still has those places. They're just a little harder to find.

## Why we're here

The Independent Web is a community for people who enjoy building,
exploring, and sharing their own corner of the internet.

That can be a personal website, a blog, an open-source project,
a small tool, an experiment, or simply an idea you'd like to share.

You don't need a large audience. You don't need to be an expert.
You just need something you're interested in.

## What you can do here

- Share your website or project.
- Discover interesting corners of the web.
- Write about something you've learned.
- Meet people building their own things.
- Explore open source and alternative ways of using the internet.

This community is still small, and that's part of the idea.

We're not trying to build another giant platform.

We're building a small place where people can **discover, share,
and build**.

## Welcome

If you're interested in the idea, come join us.
